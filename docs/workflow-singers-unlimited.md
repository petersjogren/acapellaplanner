# Singers Unlimited workflow

How Gene Puerling’s studio stacked voices — and how this booth maps that method without becoming a DAW.

## How they stacked

Singers Unlimited recorded as a small vocal group, then built a choir by overdubbing.

- **Bonnie Herman’s ghost** carried the song. She sang the lead with the real text, vowels, consonants, rubato, and intention. That performance was the feel everyone locked to — not a click, not a piano reduction.
- **Many doubles per part.** Inner lines and outer sparkle were sung again and again until the chord bloomed. Density came from humans, not chorusing plugins.
- **Lock to the ghost’s feel.** Later passes matched her vowels, breaths, and time — including the places she leaned or waited.
- **No auto-tune.** Intonation was ensemble craft: listen, match, sing again. Breath, vibrato, and consonants stayed human.

The singer in that room did not navigate a session. They heard the ghost (and later the stack), sang the line, and trusted the producer to keep what bloomed.

## How this app maps

The booth is that room. The preparer is the producer-arranger. Audio time on the ghost is the only clock.

### Ghost timeline is truth

Every phrase lives as start and end on the ghost. *In time* and *Follow the ghost* are feels on a **run of phrases** — never a second clock the singer has to fight. A section is those phrases, sung that way. A click, when used, only ticks inside an in-time section. **Play ghost** opens at 0 so the first **New phrase** tap ends that first phrase; double-click empty space fills a gap. Phrase marks sit back to back; the 2 s head start and 2 s crossfade tail are the play/record window, not the phrase boundary — those windows still overlap because of the rolls. Set either to 0 in the phrase editor if you do not want it; existing marks keep whatever they already stored.

Headphones are the score. Sheet crops and lyric text are cues. Timing comes from what you hear.

### Rubato vs in time

| Feel | Preparer sets | Singer hears |
|------|----------------|--------------|
| **Follow the ghost** | Rubato section on a phrase span; click off | Ghost phrasing only — no metronome |
| **In time** | Section on a phrase span with a tempo; optional click | Ghost plus click *only* on those phrases |

Mark sections as runs of phrases the way the arrangement actually breathes: a free intro, a locked verse, a held tag.

### Keepers

Takes save as you sing. In **Review**, promote the ones that lock into **keepers**. Keepers are the stack: later doubles hear them, the matrix counts them, export carries them. Scratch takes stay for another pass. The singer does not rate; they only mark **Good enough** when a phrase-part can rest.

### Mix presets (Headphones)

Three recipes, no mixer:

- **Ghost Focus** — ghost full; keepers silent. First doubles, matching vowels and time.
- **Stack Build** — ghost a little down; keepers up. Later doubles, sitting in the choir.
- **Blend Check** — ghost muted; keepers only. Listen whether the stack holds without the guide.

Switch anytime. The booth remembers what you heard on each take.

### Play (follow the sheet)

**Play** is not the booth. The ghost runs top to bottom (or loops one phrase / one section) while the sheet hops with the playhead. Same three headphone recipes. Tap a phrase to jump. Nothing records.

### What the singer never does

- Set loop points or scrub to find a phrase
- Arm inputs or name files
- Zoom or edit audio
- Manage routing or a mixer
- Fight a click on a rubato line
- Auto-tune, quantize, or nudge clips

They pick a part, hear the ghost and the stack, sing when the line comes, and press **Good enough** or **Next**.

## Dogfood song (30 seconds, no bundled audio)

Skip committing a silent wav. Use a real 30-second ghost instead:

1. Record or import ~30s of one lead vocal — a ballad fragment is enough.
2. Three phrases, e.g. *when I fall* (rubato, ~0–8s), *in love with you* (in time, ~8–20s), tag (~20–30s, follow the ghost).
3. Three parts (Alto, Tenor, Bass) with 2–4 doubles each.
4. Booth: Ghost Focus for the first keeper on each phrase, Stack Build for doubles, Blend Check before you leave the phrase.
5. Review keepers; export the zip.

If the stack locks by ear without nudging anything, the workflow is working.
